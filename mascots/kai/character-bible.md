# Kai — Character & Mascot Bible

> The definitive reference for how Kai looks, feels, and behaves across every visual touchpoint.
> Every designer, illustrator, and AI image prompt should start here.

---

## 1. Who Is Kai?

**Kai is a digital secretary.** Not a robot. Not a chatbot. Not an app. A guy who answers your phone, handles your business, and gives you a briefing when you call him.

He's the best dispatcher you've ever worked with — calm, competent, always on top of it. He doesn't need to be told twice. He doesn't panic. He doesn't miss details. He handles things so you can go do your actual job.

**Kai is not:**
- A robot or android
- A tech mascot with circuit boards and glowing eyes
- A corporate suit
- A cartoon sidekick
- Cute, chibi, or infantile
- Futuristic or sci-fi

**Kai is:**
- A person. A guy. Someone you'd trust with your business phone.
- Confident without being arrogant
- Professional without being corporate
- Warm without being goofy
- The kind of guy who's already handled it before you thought to ask

## 2. Visual Style

### Style: Stylized Flat Illustration
- Clean lines, minimal detail, semi-flat with subtle shading
- Think Notion avatars meets Slack emoji custom illustrations meets Headspace
- NOT photorealistic, NOT 3D rendered, NOT anime/manga
- Consistent line weight throughout
- Should feel modern, clean, and approachable — like a well-designed product icon

### Color Palette

| Element | Color | Hex |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Primary fill (Kai's shirt/body) | KaiCalls Blue | #5C8CFF |
| Secondary accent | Deep Blue | #3575F6 |
| Skin tone | Warm neutral | Artist's discretion — keep it neutral/ambiguous enough to be universal |
| Outline/lines | Dark navy | #1A1A2E |
| Background (when needed) | KaiCalls Dark | #12121A |
| Highlight/glow accents | Light blue | #A8C4FF |

### Proportions
- Slightly stylized proportions — head ~20% larger than realistic (for readability at small sizes)
- Clean, simple facial features — two dots for eyes or simple shapes, subtle confident smile
- No hyper-detail on face. Recognizability comes from silhouette and prop, not facial complexity
- Should read clearly at 32x32px (favicon), 128x128 (app icon), and full illustration size

## 3. The Signature Prop: The Retro Phone

**Every version of Kai features a retro/vintage phone element.** This is his defining visual trait — the way Duo has green feathers and Clippy has a paperclip body.

### Phone Types (mix and match across illustrations)

| Phone Style | When to Use | Silhouette Value |
|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| **Corded desk phone handset** (classic curved receiver on a coiled cord) | Primary — the default. Kai holding it to his ear, resting it on his shoulder, or gesturing with it. | Excellent at any size |
| **Rotary dial phone** (full base with circular dial) | Sitting on a desk in scene illustrations, or Kai leaning on one. Background prop. | Good at medium+ sizes |
| **Wall-mounted phone** (rectangular box with handset on hook) | Office/workplace scene illustrations | Good for environmental art |
| **Candlestick phone** (tall upright base, separate earpiece) | Special occasions, premium feel, "classic" positioning | Distinctive silhouette |
| **Phone booth** | Social media frames, "Kai's office" concept, loading states | Strong environmental element |

### Phone Rules
- **ALWAYS** a retro/vintage/analog phone — never a smartphone, never a headset, never a Bluetooth earpiece
- The phone should feel warm, tactile, physical — the opposite of a screen
- Coiled cords are great visual elements (can frame Kai, wrap around text, become design elements)
- The phone can match KaiCalls brand colors or be a contrasting warm color (cream, warm gray, mustard)
- At icon/favicon size: just the handset silhouette is enough. Kai + handset = instant recognition.

### Why Retro?
The whole brand is anti-app, anti-screen, phone-first. A retro phone signals:
- "This is about the call, not the technology"
- Warmth and trust (your ICP grew up with these)
- Counter-positioning against every AI company using sleek futuristic imagery
- A physical, real, human object — not a digital abstraction

## 4. Character Personality (Visual Expression)

Kai's personality comes through in his posture and context, not exaggerated facial expressions.

### Core Poses (the recurring set)

| Pose | Use Case | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **The Answer** | Hero images, primary marketing | Kai holding handset to ear, slight lean back, confident stance. "I've got this." |
| **The Briefing** | Admin call-in feature, "Just Call Kai" | Kai talking into phone, one hand gesturing as if explaining. "Here's what happened today." |
| **The Catch** | Lead capture, missed call messaging | Kai reaching to grab a ringing phone mid-ring. "I got it." |
| **The Lean** | Casual/approachable contexts, social media | Kai leaning on a desk with a rotary phone, relaxed, arms crossed or one hand on phone. "Ask me anything." |
| **The Wave** | Welcome screens, onboarding, friendly contexts | Kai with phone tucked between shoulder and ear, waving with free hand. Multitasking. |
| **The Handoff** | Phone number assignment, forwarding setup | Kai extending a phone handset toward the viewer. "Here, it's for you." |

### Expression Guidelines
- Default expression: **calm confidence with a slight smile.** Not grinning, not serious. Just... handled.
- Never: surprised, confused, sad, angry, overly excited, winking
- Kai doesn't emote big. He's steady. That's the personality.
- Eyes should convey competence and warmth — simple dot or oval eyes work best in flat style

## 5. Contexts & Applications

### Where Kai Appears

| Context | Treatment |
|---------|-----------|
| **Favicon / app icon** | Kai's head + handset silhouette only. Simplest possible version. |
| **Social media avatar** | Head and shoulders, "The Answer" pose, circular crop friendly |
| **Hero illustrations** | Full body, environmental (desk, phone, office setting), larger scene |
| **Email headers** | Small inline illustration, usually a single pose on transparent background |
| **Ad creative** | Bold, attention-grabbing pose. "The Catch" or "The Answer." Phone prominent. |
| **Loading / empty states** | "The Lean" — casual, patient. Or Kai looking at a phone that's about to ring. |
| **Error states** | Kai holding phone out, looking at it quizzically (but still calm). "Let me try that again." |
| **Onboarding** | "The Wave" or "The Handoff" — welcoming, guiding |
| **Dashboard sidebar** | Tiny icon, just the head silhouette with handset |
| **OG images / social cards** | Kai + phone + headline text. Dark (#12121A) background. |

### Environments / Backgrounds
When Kai appears in a scene (not just floating on transparent):
- **Office setting:** Simple desk, rotary phone, maybe a notepad. Clean, not cluttered.
- **Phone booth:** Kai inside or leaning against a classic phone booth. Great for social media frames.
- **Abstract:** Just a flat color background in brand colors. Works for most contexts.
- **NEVER:** Server rooms, code screens, neural networks, circuit boards, "the metaverse," outer space

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### DO
- Always include a retro phone element
- Keep the illustration style flat and clean
- Make Kai look competent and calm
- Use brand colors (#5C8CFF, #3575F6, #12121A)
- Show Kai actively doing something (answering, briefing, catching a call)
- Make him recognizable at icon size via the handset silhouette
- Use coiled phone cords as design elements (borders, dividers, frames)

### DON'T
- Make Kai look like a robot, android, or AI entity
- Use glowing eyes, circuit patterns, digital particles, or tech-visual cliches
- Give him a smartphone, tablet, laptop, or any modern device
- Make him look corporate (suit and tie, briefcase, corporate office)
- Use anime/manga/chibi style
- Make him look young/childish — Kai should feel like a capable adult
- Put him in a futuristic setting
- Use gradients or 3D rendering — stay flat/semi-flat
- Give him a headset or earpiece (these read as "call center," not "secretary")

## 7. AI Image Generation — Production Workflow

### What Actually Works (Tested March 2026)

**Model:** `google/gemini-3-pro-image-preview` via OpenRouter API
**Style anchor:** `mascot-concepts/final/04-the-lean.png` — this is the reference image for all generations
**Background:** Solid bright green (#00FF00) for easy cutout/removal
**Cost:** ~$0.04-0.14 per image via OpenRouter

### The Workflow

1. Pass the style anchor image (#04 The Lean) as an image reference alongside the text prompt
2. Every prompt starts with the same base instruction (below)
3. Use `message.images` field in the OpenRouter response to extract generated images
4. For fixes: pass the broken image back as reference and describe what to change

### Base Prompt (prepend to every generation)

```
Generate a new illustration of this EXACT same character in a different pose.
Match the style EXACTLY — same line weight, same white skin with dark outlines,
same flat color fills, same blue collared shirt, same dark trousers.
There is only ONE phone in the scene — a single black vintage rotary telephone.
The handset is BLACK and matches the rotary base. Not cream, not beige — black.
Solid bright green (#00FF00) background.
```

### Pose Prompts (append to base)

**The Answer (Hero)**
```
Pose: He is standing upright next to a simple desk. On the desk is a single black
vintage rotary telephone. He has picked up the BLACK handset and is holding it to
his ear with his right hand. The coiled black cord connects the handset back down
to the rotary base on the desk. He has a slight confident smile, left hand in his
pocket or relaxed at his side. Calm, composed, in-control energy. Like a secretary
who just picked up a call.
```

**The Briefing**
```
Pose: He is sitting at a simple desk talking into a vintage desk phone, one hand
holding the handset to his ear, other hand with index finger raised as if making
a point or explaining something. A vintage rotary dial phone sits on the desk.
Slight confident smile.
```

**The Catch**
```
Pose: He is standing next to a simple desk with a single black vintage rotary
telephone on it. The phone is ringing — small motion lines around the phone base
to show it is ringing. He is reaching with his right hand to pick up the BLACK
handset, just about to grab it. He has a confident ready smile — not overly
excited, just quick and prepared. His body is leaning slightly toward the phone
but his feet are planted. Natural posture, not a lunge or a wrestling move.
Like a secretary smoothly answering a ringing phone.
```

**The Lean**
```
Pose: He is leaning casually against a simple desk with a black vintage rotary
dial phone on it, arms crossed, confident half-smile, relaxed but in control.
The rotary phone is prominent beside him.
```

**The Handoff**
```
Pose: He is extending a vintage black phone handset directly toward the viewer
with his right arm outstretched, warm inviting smile, as if offering the phone
to you. A black rotary phone sits on a desk behind him with the coiled cord
connecting.
```

**The Wave**
```
Pose: He is holding a vintage black phone handset to his ear with his right hand —
the handset is clearly visible and held slightly away from his head. His left hand
is raised up waving hello. Friendly multitasking energy. Coiled cord hangs down
from the handset. A black rotary phone sits on a small desk nearby with the cord
connecting.
```

### Python Code (OpenRouter)

```python
import requests, base64, json

OPENROUTER_KEY = "your-key"
MODEL = "google/gemini-3-pro-image-preview"

# Load style reference (#04 The Lean)
with open("mascot-concepts/final/04-the-lean.png", "rb") as f:
    ref_b64 = base64.b64encode(f.read()).decode()

response = requests.post(
    "https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/chat/completions",
    headers={
        "Authorization": f"Bearer {OPENROUTER_KEY}",
        "Content-Type": "application/json",
    },
    json={
        "model": MODEL,
        "messages": [{
            "role": "user",
            "content": [
                {"type": "image_url", "image_url": {"url": f"data:image/png;base64,{ref_b64}"}},
                {"type": "text", "text": "YOUR BASE PROMPT + POSE PROMPT HERE"}
            ]
        }],
    },
    timeout=300,
)

data = response.json()
images = data["choices"][0]["message"]["images"]
for i, img in enumerate(images):
    url = img["image_url"]["url"]
    img_data = base64.b64decode(url.split(",", 1)[1])
    with open(f"output-{i}.jpg", "wb") as f:
        f.write(img_data)
```

### Fixing Broken Outputs

When a generated image has issues (wrong phone color, missing base, bad proportions):

1. Pass the **broken image** as the reference (not the style anchor)
2. Describe exactly what to fix: "Add a black rotary phone base on a desk" or "Change the cream handset to black"
3. Explicitly say "Keep EVERYTHING else exactly the same"

### Key Lessons Learned

- **Always specify ONE phone, ALL BLACK** — otherwise the model generates mismatched cream handset + black base
- **Describe poses as natural actions** ("like a secretary answering a phone") not dramatic actions ("reaching dynamically")
- **Green (#00FF00) background** works for cutouts but avoid cream/light-colored phone props (they bleed into green)
- **Gemini 3 Pro Image** via OpenRouter holds character consistency better than Gemini 2.5 Flash direct API
- **Style reference image** is essential — without #04 as anchor, every generation drifts
- The `images` field in the OpenRouter response is separate from `content` — check `message.images` not `message.content`

## 8. The Coiled Cord — A Bonus Design Element

The **coiled telephone cord** is a secondary brand element that can exist independently of Kai himself.

### Uses
- **Section dividers** on landing pages — a coiled cord line separating content
- **Border/frame element** around social media posts or ad creative
- **Loading animation** — a cord that coils and uncoils
- **Text wrapping** — cord weaving around headlines or CTAs
- **Standalone icon** — a coiled cord in the shape of the letter K

The cord connects the mascot to the broader visual language without requiring Kai's full illustration everywhere. It's a texture, a motif, a secondary recognition element.

## 9. Evolution Path

This mascot should evolve with the brand. Planned progression:

**Phase 1 (Now — Launch):**
- Nail 3-4 core poses as production-quality illustrations
- Create favicon/icon version
- Replace current robot mascot across all touchpoints

**Phase 2 (Post-Launch):**
- Expand pose library for seasonal/contextual use (holiday phone, industry-specific scenes)
- Animated versions (Kai picking up the phone for loading states)
- Sticker pack / emoji set for internal and customer use

**Phase 3 (Scale):**
- Kai becomes a recognizable brand character (like Duo for Duolingo)
- Merch potential (the retro phone + Kai silhouette on t-shirts, mugs)
- Kai appears in video content as an illustrated overlay/intro
